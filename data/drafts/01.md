Introducing Box Architect: frontier AI to help teams design, build and improve workflows around their enterprise content.

Describe a process or goal in plain language. Architect will use the content and procedures already in Box to define the stages, decisions and hand-offs, then assemble the agents, apps, automations and forms the process needs.

Teams will be able to test workflows with real content before deployment, choose models for individual steps and add human review at key decisions.

Architect will then monitor results and recommend improvements for process owners to review.
