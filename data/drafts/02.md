Author: Aaron Levie

Deploying AI into the economy is the opportunity right now, and almost nobody is pricing in how much work it is.

Swapping out enterprise workflows always takes far more effort than anyone expects. That's what the applied layer of AI really looks like: software and agents, plus a big new opening for services firms.

Think about the list. Move legacy systems to the cloud. Fix how data is organized and accessed. Wire software up to agents. Redesign workflows around agents. Work out human-in-the-loop. Build and maintain evals. Then keep updating all of it every time a new model ships.

This isn't classic software deployment. With software, you implemented a known category, stepped back, and the customer ran it. With agents you're delivering actual work output inside a process. That's a completely different implementation job.

So expect a wave of new firms, by industry, by company size and by problem, plus SIs that modernize (and some that won't). Great moment to be an FDE, or to build an FDE firm.
