Author: Annabel Gray

I was recently fortunate enough to attend Profound's Zero Click World Tour in Sydney.

I am very excited about AI and the potential - but I do try to be level-headed about it and try to ensure I'm not in an echo chamber of my own creation.

So I went into Profound with an open mind, wondering whether they would prove that AI search really is the big deal I keep saying it is.

I didn't last long - they had me at the opening credits. Great graphics!

Aside from the graphics, there were some genuinely brilliant, practical insights and presentations from a fantastic lineup of speakers, including - Destiny Flaherty (Princess Polly), Angela Feng (Atlassian), Luciano Viterale (Rippling), Bianca Shah and Kiefer Casamore (ahm and TBWA\Australia), and Angela Grant and Liam Kirk (Virgin Australia).

I've pulled together my key takeaways from the event, including what brands should be thinking about across onsite optimisation, offsite visibility, affiliate partnerships and the next wave of AI agents.

This article is for anyone who missed the event or is keen to get across what industry leaders are doing in this space.

And a huge thank you to the team at Zero Click by Profound for putting on such a thought-provoking event, and to everyone who shared their experiences.

Bring on the future! 🤖 ✨
