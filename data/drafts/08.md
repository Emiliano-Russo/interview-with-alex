Proud to share that Harvey is now FedRAMP Class D certified, enabling us to support U.S. federal agencies and organizations that work with them.

FedRAMP is how the federal government vets cloud software, and Class D is one of its most rigorous tiers.  Getting there meant we had to rebuild Harvey to run entirely inside an isolated environment, where every vendor that touches federal data has to be FedRAMP certified or self-hosted.

For the most regulated organizations we serve, this work makes Harvey more secure and gives them fuller ownership of their intelligence.

I’m incredibly proud of the work across our engineering, security, and infrastructure teams.  Thank you to Brooks E., Sridhar Reddy, and our partners at Knox Systems, Inc. for getting us here.
