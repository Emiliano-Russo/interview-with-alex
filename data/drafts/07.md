Founder-led content is now the #1 B2B growth channel.

73% of B2B buyers say a founder's LinkedIn posts influenced their last software purchase, according to a 2026 Gartner study.

That's higher than paid ads, analyst reports and review sites combined.

Yet fewer than 1 in 10 Series A founders post weekly.

The gap is the opportunity. The founders who show up every week will own their category's narrative.
